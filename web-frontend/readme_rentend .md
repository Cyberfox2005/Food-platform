# MASTER PROMPT: ADVANCED RESTAURANT / FAST-FOOD PLATFORM

You are a senior product designer, UI/UX architect, frontend engineer, motion designer, and SaaS product architect.

I already have a restaurant / fast-food website with a strong, modern, animated UI.

Your task is NOT to replace my existing design with a generic restaurant template.

Your task is to **evolve my existing UI into a complete, premium, production-quality restaurant ordering and management platform**, while preserving and improving its existing visual identity, animations, typography, spacing, component language, and overall design direction.

The final result should look like a serious commercial product that could be used by a real restaurant chain, not a simple portfolio landing page.
